Day 2 of 3

Saturday ☀️

July 19, 2026 — The big adventure day!

✦ antiques ✦ scavenger hunt ✦ farmers market ✦ pizza ✦ movies ✦ crafts
Morning
🥐 Light Breakfast
Wake up slowly (no rush!), grab a light bite — pastries, fruit, coffee, juice. Check the Breakfast menu for the full spread. Chat over coffee, ease into the day 🌤️
Mid-Morning
🌿 Kensington Farmers Market
We head to Kensington! The farmers market is a magical little spot — fresh flowers, local produce, handmade goodies, and the perfect Saturday morning energy. Grab a coffee, browse the stalls, soak it all in 🌸

Address: Howard Avenue, Kensington, MD
Mid-Morning
🏺 Antique Row Scavenger Hunt!
The main event for the wedding party! We split into two teams of five and compete along Howard Avenue's legendary Antique Row. Hunt for clues, find treasures, and earn points at shops like The Cusp of Extinction, Goldsborough Glynn, M.O.B. Vintage, Joyful Heart Reclaimed, and West Howard Antiques.

May the best team win... 👀
Late Morning / Early Afternoon
🍷 Wine & Wind Down
After the hunt, pop into Knowles Station Wine & Co for a little wind-down moment — wine, good company, celebrate the winning scavenger hunt team 🏆
Afternoon / Lunch
🍕 Frankly… Pizza
Lunch at Frankly… Pizza in Kensington! Late pizza lunch for the crew — because nothing hits better after a morning of treasure hunting than a great slice.

Pro tip: Come hungry 🍕
Afternoon
🏠 Back to Home Base
We head back to the house, change into PJs, and shift into full cozy mode. The rest of Saturday belongs to us — no schedules, no rushing.
Evening
🎬 Movie Night + Crafts
This is what it's all about — PJs on, snacks out, craft supplies ready, and the movie queue loaded. Vote for your picks on the Movie Night page! We'll do crafts while watching — the perfect cozy sleepover energy 🎨✨

🗺️ Scavenger Hunt Highlights

🦴 The Cusp of Extinction 💍 Goldsborough Glynn 👗 M.O.B. Vintage 🌿 Joyful Heart Reclaimed 🏛️ West Howard Antiques 🍽️ The Dish & Dram 🍷 Knowles Station Wine

💡 Saturday Tips

  • Wear comfy shoes — we'll be walking Antique Row!
  • Dress code transition: Farmer's Market Chic in the AM → PJs by evening 🌻
  • Bring a tote bag for any market finds 🛍️
  • Don't forget to vote for the movie before Saturday night → Movie Night
